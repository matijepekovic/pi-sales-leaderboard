MOD Resulting instructions

Fill out your sheets, send results, and pull the Redlines PDF and OLY Calc.

On this page Start here Top controls Fill in a sheet Send results Canceled & locked sheets Progress & retries Export reports Troubleshooting

Start here

  1. Pick the day’s MOD. If a sheet is missing or the appointment changed in Salesforce, hit Refresh.
  2. Open the right sheet and fill in the Dispo, products, call times, Notes, and redlines that apply.
  3. Hit Result on one Work Order at a time. Check the bell for Submitted.
  4. Open PDFs when you’re ready to pull the reports.

Skip the batch if you can. Shit can still go wrong halfway through. Sending one at a time makes it easier to see what went through and what you need to retry.

ResultSend one Work Order to Salesforce.
Finish for PDFSave a Salesforce cancellation for the Redlines PDF only.
OverrideReopen a completed sheet for full editing. A later submission can update Salesforce.
Edit PDF notesAdd text to the next Redlines PDF while the canceled sheet stays locked.

Top controls

Search customer or rep
Filter the display by customer name or assigned rep. Clear the search to see all sheets. Search only changes what you see.
MOD
Select the manager responsible for the day before using Result, End of day, or Finish for PDF. The first successful Salesforce submission locks that day’s MOD selection.
Hide completed
Hide sheets that are already completed. Sheets with a draft Dispo, a pending submission, or a failed submission stay visible. Uncheck to see completed sheets again.
Refresh
Save in-progress edits and read the latest Salesforce data. Use this after an appointment is canceled, added, or changed. A browser reload alone doesn’t pull Salesforce again.
Clear
Clear local edits wipes the day’s local edits, including Notes and redlines, and reloads the values from Salesforce. Use it only if you mean to throw those edits away.
End of day
This is the batch button. It can send all eligible sheets or just the ones you pick. Use Result on each sheet for your normal workflow.
Notification bell
Open Resulting notifications to see what went through, what’s still running, and what failed. The badge shows unread updates.
PDFs
Open the Redlines and OLY Calc editors. If no sheets are loaded, hit Refresh. If the button is disabled, check for sheets still missing a Dispo or product result, including sheets hidden by a filter.
Info
Open this guide in a new tab. Your MOD sheets stay open in the original tab.

Fill in a MOD sheet

The customer, address, reps, and appointment details come from Salesforce. MOD saves a local draft as you go. Hit Done in a popup to get back to the sheet. Hit Result when you’re ready to send it.

Dispo and products

  • Pick the main Dispo from Sale, Pitch No Sale, No Pitch, CXL (Canceled), NRA (No Rep Available), or OOS (Out of Scope).
  • For a Sale, open Products, check each product that sold, and enter its Sale price, Deposit (use 0 if none), and optional Payment. A blank or zero sale price doesn’t count as sold. The first checked product with a positive price is primary.
  • For a sheet with multiple products and a main Dispo other than Sale, check Result individually and open Products to give the products different outcomes. Choose the Primary product, then fill in each product’s Result and Result Detail. For each product marked Sale, enter the price and deposit. Use 0 if there’s no deposit. Payment is optional. A product without an individual outcome uses the main Dispo.
  • Click Result Detail for the main reason or to check CXL At Lead. The CXL At Lead checkbox is separate from selecting CXL as Dispo.

Notes, numbers, and red ink

Numbers
Enter quoted amounts here. The highest amount of 100 or more becomes Start Price and the lowest becomes Final Price. With 95, 18,000, and 16,000, Start is 18,000 and Final is 16,000.
MOD Notes
Put your regular notes here. The counter covers Numbers + Notes together because both go into Salesforce MOD Notes. If you’re over the limit, trim the text before you hit Result.
Red pen
Click the small red pen at the bottom left of the Numbers area. Enter redlines and click Done. On a normal submission, manual redlines go to the comment on every product being resulted. They also print in red on the MOD sheet.
Work Order amounts
Click Start Price, Final Price, or Deposit/Payment to enter a manual value. Clear a manual value to use the automatic amount again. For Deposit/Payment, enter the two amounts separated by a slash, such as 500/239.50. For cash, the payment can be left blank, as in 500/.

Call times, checklist, and DNR

  • For Call 1 and Call 2, type a time with AM or PM. Tap the clock for the current time or hold or right-click it to pick an exact time. Use Set time to save it or Clear inside that picker to remove it.
  • Fill in Need and Want. Check whichever apply from Power Questions, T Close, Docs, Bid Sheets, Promo, Review, Pictures, 1 Leg, Free Lead, and 90 Min.
  • Where available, hold or right-click a source-backed checkbox to inspect its stored value. If the editor offers Save value, you can set the exact value on an editable sheet. Power Questions, T Close, Bid Sheets, and Pictures are local MOD checkmarks.
  • DNR opens Do Not Reset. Choose every reason that applies. Use Reasons to reopen the list. If you close a newly checked DNR without choosing a reason, the checkbox clears.

Send results to Salesforce

One at a time
Check the Work Order and its Dispo. Hit Result, check the Work Order number in the prompt, then hit Yes. Look for Submitted in the bell.
If you need a batch
Go to End of day → Yes → Submit Selected. Pick the Work Orders and hit Submit Selected again. You can search by customer or Work Order number, or use Select All and Clear All.
Submit All
End of day → Yes → Submit All processes every eligible sheet, even ones hidden by your filters. If a batch goes sideways, check each Work Order in the bell before you retry anything.

A queued sheet is still processing. The completed label in the bell tells you whether it was sent to Salesforce or saved locally for PDF.

Canceled and locked sheets

Salesforce canceled the appointment

Click Refresh. On an unfinished card labeled Canceled in Salesforce · Redlines PDF only, add your Notes and redlines. The Dispo is locked. Hit Finish for PDF, then Yes. The bell shows Saved for PDF. That action sends no Salesforce update.

You’re choosing CXL in MOD Resulting

Selecting CXL in the Dispo menu is a normal result. Clicking Result or submitting it through End of day uses the normal Salesforce path.

The sheet is already locked

On a locked canceled or CXL sheet, hit Edit PDF notes. Enter Additional Notes and Additional redlines, then hit Save. Pull a fresh Redlines PDF to see what you added. The sheet stays locked and the additions go only to the PDF.

Override is for reopening the entire sheet when you intend to correct a result. Override itself unlocks the card. A later Result or End of day on a normal card can send its result, Dispo, and other mapped fields to Salesforce.

Progress and retries

Open the Resulting notifications bell and read the status for the specific Work Order.

Queued / In progress
It’s still running. Wait for a final status before you retry.
Submitted
The Salesforce submission completed. The sheet locks and is excluded from later batches unless reopened.
Saved for PDF
The canceled sheet was completed locally and its documentation is available for the Redlines PDF.
Rejected
Read the error, fix the issue, and use Result on that Work Order.
Needs verification
MOD doesn’t know yet if it went through. If you retry, MOD checks the first attempt before it sends anything again. Give that check time to finish.
Not submitted
MOD stopped before sending this Work Order. Read the error, then retry that Work Order on its own when ready.

If a submission reports that the appointment was canceled in Salesforce, click Refresh and look for Finish for PDF on the affected sheet.

Export reports

Reports use the saved MOD sheets. You can generate them as soon as every sheet has an outcome, even before End of day. Choosing or confirming either report doesn’t submit results to Salesforce.

Redlines PDF

  1. Open PDFs → Redlines (MOD Sheets + Cover Page).
  2. Review the OLY MOD Cover. Edit sale rows, the Daily Summary, Grand Total, or Daily Totals as needed. Use Add row in the appropriate section or Remove next to a row.
  3. Click Confirm or Confirm cover and create Redlines PDF. When ready, click Open Redlines PDF.

Automatic cover totals exclude CXL and OOS. Their individual MOD sheets still appear in the PDF. After saving more Notes or redlines on a sheet, start a fresh report from PDFs to include them.

OLY Calc PNG

  1. Open PDFs → OLY Calc. You can also open OLY Calc from the Redlines cover editor.
  2. Review the inputs and calculated results. Changing an input recalculates dependent values. You can also type a manual value into a calculated result.
  3. Click Confirm or Confirm OLY Calc and generate PNG. When ready, click Download OLY Calc PNG.

The automatic Leads count excludes CXL and OOS. The image footer shows the date.

Troubleshooting

I can’t find a Work Order.

Clear Search and uncheck Hide completed. If the list has changed in Salesforce, click the MOD page’s Refresh.

Only Override is available.

The sheet is locked. If Salesforce just canceled the appointment, use Refresh first. On a locked canceled or CXL sheet, use Edit PDF notes to add text to the Redlines PDF.

The Notes counter is over the limit.

Shorten regular Notes or the Numbers text and check the counter before submitting. The separate red pen text doesn’t use that MOD Notes limit.

The export doesn’t include my latest edits.

Return to MOD Sheets, make sure your edits have saved, and start a fresh report from PDFs. In the report editor, click Confirm again after making report edits.
