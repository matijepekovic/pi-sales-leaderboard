Uses the same Gmail account, mailbox and email-check interval. Leave email collection enabled. The gallery keyword is separate from print filters: existing printing still happens whenever its own filters match. Gallery imports neither add prints nor cancel them.

One PNG starts at each form's top border and ends just before the next form's top border. The last runs to the page bottom; full side margins stay. No equal thirds, adjustable margins or OCR for cutting. Text recognition is used afterward for search and the printed date.

Retention removes expired images and their notes. Uncertain dates remain until corrected in the gallery. PDFs and full-page renders are temporary and discarded after processing; unrecognized pages are listed in import status, not stored as full images. This does not remove files needed by a print job. Imports stop accepting more files before the gallery budget or 512 MB free-disk reserve is exhausted.
